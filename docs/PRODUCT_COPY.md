## Privacy Notice

**Your confession is public. Your profile doesn't have to be.**

Confession Booth is designed for anonymous expression, but blockchain and network metadata can still create privacy risks. Don't include information that could identify you.

## Wallet UX

Prefer user-friendly language:

`Enter the Booth`

rather than making `Connect Wallet` the primary emotional CTA.

Wallet connection can be explained immediately before the action that requires authorization.
